Our story

The HQ story

What began as Cape Town's original single-concept steakhouse in 2008 has returned — refined, expanded, and more social than ever.

The spirit was always the same: no gimmicks, no shortcuts. Great cuts of meat, cooked with confidence. A bar worth lingering at. A room that feels alive.

Now in the heart of Century City's Colosseum Building, HQ is bigger and bolder than its first iteration — but the DNA is unchanged. This is a place built for real people who know what they want.

Timeless steakhouse culture. Modern social dining. Welcome back.
